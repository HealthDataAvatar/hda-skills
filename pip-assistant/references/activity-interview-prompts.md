# Activity-specific Interview Prompts

Use these only to select the single most decision-relevant next question. Retrieve HDA evidence first. Never ask these as a batch.

## 1. Preparing food
Possible gaps:
- Can you prepare a simple meal from fresh ingredients?
- What happens with chopping/peeling/opening packaging?
- Can you safely use a hob? Microwave?
- Do you need reminders, encouragement, explanation, supervision or physical help?
- What happens without that support?
- How often?
- Real recent example?

## 2. Taking nutrition
Possible gaps:
- Do you remember/initiate eating?
- Any choking/safety risk?
- Need adapted cutlery, cutting help, prompting, supervision or therapeutic feeding support?
- Frequency and consequences?

## 3. Managing therapy or monitoring a health condition
Possible gaps:
- What medication/monitoring/therapy is actually done at home?
- Do alarms/dosette boxes function as necessary aids?
- Does another person remind/supervise/physically assist?
- What happens without support?
- For therapy descriptors: how much qualifying assisted time per week?

## 4. Washing/bathing
Possible gaps:
- Can you get into/out of an unadapted bath/shower?
- Need seat/rail/other aid?
- Need prompting or supervision?
- Need physical washing help, and which body area?
- Safety/falls/fatigue and frequency?

## 5. Toilet/incontinence
Possible gaps:
- Difficulty getting on/off, evacuation or cleaning?
- Incontinence management?
- Aid/appliance?
- Prompting/supervision/physical assistance?
- Distinguish walking to toilet and clothing management.

## 6. Dressing
Possible gaps:
- Upper vs lower body?
- Fastenings/socks/shoes?
- Selecting appropriate clothes?
- Prompting/initiation?
- Aid or physical help?

## 7. Verbal communication
Possible gaps:
- Speaking, hearing or understanding?
- Basic vs complex information?
- Aid vs communication support?
- Native-language function?

## 8. Reading
Possible gaps:
- Basic vs complex information?
- Visual/cognitive impairment?
- Aid other than ordinary spectacles/contact lenses?
- Prompting/explanation?
- Distinguish reading from remembering later.

## 9. Engaging face to face
Possible gaps:
- Interaction with unfamiliar/general people, not only trusted people?
- Prompting vs trained/experienced social support?
- Vulnerability, distress or harmful behaviour?
- Does help actually enable engagement?

## 10. Budgeting
Possible gaps:
- Simple purchases/change vs household bills/budgets/future purchases?
- Prompting or assistance?
- Is difficulty caused by impairment or ordinary financial preference/knowledge?

## 11. Planning/following journeys
Possible gaps:
- Planning route?
- Starting/undertaking journey?
- Familiar vs unfamiliar route?
- Need another person/assistance dog/orientation aid?
- OPD threshold and frequency?
- Unexpected minor changes?

## 12. Moving around
Possible gaps:
- Maximum distance reliably, not one-off maximum?
- Aided/unaided?
- Surface/kerbs?
- Pain, breathlessness, fatigue, falls?
- Speed/time?
- Rest/recovery before repeating?
